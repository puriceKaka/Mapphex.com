Role Portals

Every user lands in the right workspace.

One smart login checks the account role and routes users to the correct portal. Visibility stays scoped, so people only see the branches, departments, staff records, and reports they are allowed to use.

01

Director

Sees everything, creates accounts, manages access, and reviews executive reports.

02

HR

Manages employees, attendance, leave, payroll, recruitment, reviews, and disciplinary records.

03

Branch Manager

Works only inside their assigned branch: staff, inventory, sales, finance, transfers, and assets.

04

Department Head

Controls department members, budget, tasks, documents, KPIs, and department-level reports.

05

Staff

Uses self-service for their own profile, attendance, leave requests, payslips, and updates.
